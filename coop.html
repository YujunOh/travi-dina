Review notes 6 open questions · 4 assumptions

OPEN QUESTIONS

  1. How do members sign in: basic only, basic plus social, or social only (Google only, if social)? There is a concern about depending on Google. Should guests who are not signed in see this list at all?
  2. Is someone a co-op member the moment they sign up, or a general user until they join a co-op? The status line at the top changes with the answer, which is why it shows "Member of TRAVI" and "Member of a co-op" separately.
  3. Who approves a join request: the platform Admin (SFR-005 draft) or the co-op leader?
  4. What term should replace "Domain"?
  5. Should compute and model be accepted as ways to take part? One proposal shows "Model" only in co-ops that allow fine-tuning.
  6. Should a co-op where one company holds 75% or more of the data be treated as an exception, and marked in this list?

ASSUMPTIONS

  1. The mock draws only the signed-in Member state. The guest view is covered by the co-op list on the Domain pages.
  2. Co-op names, member counts, rounds and template names are invented. Member organizations are written as Org A–H, never as real organizations.
  3. The data template download and the business document upload are placeholders that do nothing.
  4. All four app screens show the same sample account on the same date, Dec 3, 2026. Org A is already in SOC Threat Detection Co-op (approved Oct 1) and has one pending request, to Smart Farm Yield Co-op (Nov 30). The Admin role is granted to this account only so the mock can show switching.
