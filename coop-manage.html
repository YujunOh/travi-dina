Review notes 7 open questions · 3 assumptions

OPEN QUESTIONS

  1. Two role layers: does each organization need its own manager role apart from the Co-op leader? Is "Co-op leader" the final name?
  2. Who executes the settlement payout: the co-op leader, TRAVI admin, or the OC?
  3. Who receives the retraining notice, and who decides to retrain?
  4. Can a company that stays refuse unlearning on its own local model? Is the model deployed to nodes, or kept only on the orchestrator?
  5. Should a co-op where one company holds 75% or more of the data be treated as an exception?
  6. Does the professor confirm Hedera as the settlement payout method? What is the payout unit (token or fiat)?
  7. Accuracy baseline: same as one pooled (monolithic) model, or just better than the local model?

ASSUMPTIONS

  1. Approve and execute-payout buttons are left out or disabled here. Their owners are not decided, so this is the most conservative form.
  2. The professor put admin dashboards at low priority for the MVP. This screen is a lo-fi review mock-up, not MVP build scope.
  3. Compute preview re-calculates the same example numbers as the receipt on the home page. It is not the real contribution scoring method.
