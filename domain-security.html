CASE STUDY

AI Cooperative in action: Cybersecurity

Small and medium businesses rarely have a security operations team, and none of them sees enough attacks alone to train a strong model. In a security Coop, they train one model together, each on its own attack data.

Members & output

MEMBERS
Small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) with no security operations team
OUTPUT
A shared attack-prediction model, trained in cohorts

Context

Breaches are rising, and Korea's ICT ministry (MSIT) is engaging with the problem.

How cohorts form

Cohorts form around similar assets, risks, and operating needs.

  • Windows and Linux environments face different threats.
  • Different assets and services expose different known vulnerabilities (CVEs).
  • Risk tolerance and security policy differ by organization.

Value alignment

Collaboration lets each organization build a model specific to itself.

  • Learn from another organization's data when it fits its own assets and policy.
  • The result is a better model tuned to its own context.

Beyond prediction, managing business risk

Every attack carries a cost in assets and operations. Predict the attack, and security becomes business protection.

In practice: ACA & CMAE

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM

Our Autonomous Cybersecurity Agent (ACA) screens incoming payloads with CMAE, a classification model we built. Real attack data accumulates per organization, and each contribution is recorded sample by sample. That record is the input Traversal Learning learns from.
