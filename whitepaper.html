Settlement on Hedera

Fair rewards need settlement that participants can check. Public blockchains make this hard for distributed learning: as SettleFL puts it, "the prohibitive cost of permissionless blockchains directly clashes with the high-frequency, iterative nature of model training."

TRAVI splits settlement into three layers. Contribution records for each round are anchored to Hedera Consensus Service, at about $0.0008 per message (Hedera fee schedule, Nov 2026). Settlement amounts are computed off-chain from contribution scores using the cooperative's sharing rule. Payouts are made on the Hedera network each settlement round.

STATUS

Record: in design. Compute: research (arXiv:2606.28217). Pay: planned. Unlearning on exit: roadmap.
