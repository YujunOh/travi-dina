Review notesOpen questions and assumptions for this screen

OPEN QUESTIONS

  1. If a member deletes their account while still in a Coop, does leaving (unlearning) start for every Coop at once, or must they leave each Coop first?
  2. Does "unlearning" include retraining? What should the processing step be called?
  3. Legal deadline for processing an exit needs checking: GDPR one month, 45 days (assumed to be CCPA), Singapore "reasonable period".
  4. How are payouts already received handled when a member leaves? A severance-style idea was rejected; the current direction is "only revenue from contribution". When does settlement end after leaving?
  5. How is contribution handled for a round a node missed because of a failure? (A failed node gets no reward; grey zone.)
  6. How does a participant connect the Hedera account that receives payouts, and what is the payout unit?

ASSUMPTIONS

  1. "Payouts already settled are not changed" is an assumption: the contribution was already paid for, and attacks already blocked do not disappear after unlearning. It is marked as an assumption in the leave dialog and needs confirmation.
  2. The Hedera payout account line is the minimum the Pay layer needs (participants paid on Hedera), so it is shown as PLANNED.
  3. You can leave only a Coop that approved you, so the Smart Farm row (request pending) has no active leave action. This page shows your own leave flow before a request is sent.

ALSO ON THIS SCREEN

  1. Hedera is treated as the means of paying settlements. This is the team's reading and is not confirmed by the professor.
  2. Whether a member becomes a Coop member at sign-up is open, so "Member of TRAVI" and "Member of <Coop>" are written separately.
  3. The screen shows several roles on one account but does not change FLARE certificate roles. This conflicts with SER-001 ("one role only") and the SER-008 proposal (role fixed at registration).

These notes list open questions and assumptions. They are not agreed decisions.
